## 1. Hypotheses

We pre-register three primary experimental hypotheses testing whether Kodiac's visual specification testing engine acts as a superior autonomous data filter for robotic self-improvement compared to status-quo VLM judges and baseline filters.

* **H1 (Label Quality / False-PASS Supression):**  
  On the 1,000-episode practice pool, Kodiac achieves a significantly lower false-PASS rate than any VLM baseline judge:
  $$\text{False-PASS}(\text{Kodiac}) < \min_{B \in \mathcal{B}} \text{False-PASS}(B)$$
  where $\mathcal{B} = \{\text{Single-pass Spark 1.3}, \text{Robometer-4B}, \text{TOPReward}, \text{Accept-all}\}$.  
  *Operational definition:* A false-PASS occurs when an episode evaluated by the judge is accepted (`verdict == PASS` or `score >= \tau`), but the physics ground truth oracle reports `oracle_success == False`.

* **H2 (Downstream Policy Competence / Headline Proof):**  
  When an imitation learning policy ($\pi$) is trained from scratch on $\{\mathcal{D}_{\text{clean}} \cup \mathcal{D}_{\text{accepted}}(J)\}$ for a fixed gradient step budget $N = 20,000$, the policy trained on Kodiac-accepted rollouts achieves higher task success on $\ge 200$ held-out evaluation environments than policies trained on baseline-accepted sets:
  $$\mathbb{E}[\text{Success}(\pi_{\text{Kodiac}})] > \mathbb{E}[\text{Success}(\pi_{\text{Spark}})] + \Delta_{\min}$$
  where $\Delta_{\min} > 0$ with statistical significance ($p < 0.05$ via Welch's $t$-test across 3 random seeds).

* **H3 (Prefix Salvage Advantage):**  
  Policies trained on Kodiac prefix-salvaged demonstrations (truncating failed trajectories at exact onset frame $t^*$) achieve downstream task success greater than or equal to policies trained only on full-episode Kodiac-accepted demonstrations:
  $$\mathbb{E}[\text{Success}(\pi_{\text{Kodiac+Salvage}})] \ge \mathbb{E}[\text{Success}(\pi_{\text{Kodiac}})]$$

## 2. Experimental Task & Environment Specification

* **Environment:** ManiSkill3 / SAPIEN 3 Panda StackCube task.
* **Observation Space:** Robot proprioception (joint positions, joint velocities, end-effector pose, gripper width) and cube poses ($x, y, z, q_w, q_x, q_y, q_z$).
* **Control Space:** Cartesian end-effector delta pose `pd_ee_delta_pos` ($dx, dy, dz, dr, dp, dy, \text{gripper}$), 7-DoF, float32, normalized to $[-1, 1]$.
* **Frequency:** 30 Hz control loop and CFR H.264 video rendering.
* **Camera Views:** Fixed third-person exterior camera ($640 \times 480$) and optional wrist camera, recorded with calibrated intrinsics $K$ and extrinsics $[R \mid t]$.

### Exact Oracle Ground-Truth Thresholds
An episode is classified as `oracle_success == True` if and only if all of the following physical criteria hold after a mandatory settling dwell ($\Delta t \ge 0.5\text{ s}$):
1. **Planar Alignment:** Horizontal Euclidean distance between Cube A and Cube B center of mass:
   $$\Delta_{xy} = \sqrt{(x_A - x_B)^2 + (y_A - y_B)^2} \le 3.33\text{ cm}$$
2. **Vertical Clearance / Stacking Contact:** Vertical separation between Cube A bottom face and Cube B top face:
   $$0.0\text{ cm} \le \Delta_z \le 0.50\text{ cm}$$
3. **Gripper Release:** The robot gripper is open and not exerting grasp contact on Cube A:
   $$\text{gripper\_width} \ge 0.06\text{ m} \quad \text{and} \quad \text{contact\_force}(\text{gripper}, \text{Cube A}) == 0$$
4. **Physical Rest / Settling Velocity:** Settling velocity check over a $0.5\text{ s}$ dwell window to prevent bounce false-positives:
   $$\|\mathbf{v}_{\text{linear}, A}\| < 0.01\text{ m/s} \quad \text{and} \quad \|\mathbf{v}_{\text{linear}, B}\| < 0.01\text{ m/s}$$

## 3. Practice Pool Generation (`pool_config.yaml`)

* **Total Episodes:** 1,000 episodes rendered at 30 Hz, split into:
  * **Calibration Split:** 200 episodes (Fit: 100, Validate: 100) — reserved strictly for tuning threshold hyper-parameters and camera uncertainty $\lambda$.
  * **Training Pool Split:** 800 episodes — used exclusively for policy retraining and comparative evaluation.
* **Data Mixture Composition:**
  * **70% (700 episodes) Weak Policy ($\pi_0$) Rollouts:** Autonomous rollouts generated by a state-based Diffusion Policy trained on 20 clean demonstrations to an intermediate checkpoint exhibiting 30%–60% task success.
  * **30% (300 episodes) Perturbed-Expert Near-Misses:** Motion-planned trajectories injected with controlled physical perturbations:
    * *Early open* (weight 0.25): Gripper opens at 60%–85% path progress.
    * *XY offset* (weight 0.25): Target placement offset $U(1.0, 6.0)\text{ cm}$.
    * *Low lift* (weight 0.20): Vertical lift limited to $U(1.0, 3.0)\text{ cm}$ (nominal 10.0 cm).
    * *Short dwell* (weight 0.15): Gripper releases before settling ($U(0.0, 0.2)\text{ s}$).
    * *Regrasp / Slip* (weight 0.15): Slip during transport causing rotational misalignment.
* **Storage Schema (`.state.npz`):**
  * `actions`: `np.ndarray (T, 7)` in `pd_ee_delta_pos` space.
  * `obs_state`: `np.ndarray (T, d)` containing proprioception and object states.
  * `cam_K`: `(3, 3)` float32 camera intrinsic matrix.
  * `cam_extrinsic`: `(4, 4)` float32 camera extrinsic matrix.
  * `is_mock`: `bool` indicating whether generated via kinematic mock or true physics.

## 4. Benchmark Arms

We evaluate 10 pre-registered experimental arms on the exact same practice pool:

1. **Oracle (Gold Upper Bound):** Ground-truth physics simulator verdict (`oracle_success`).
2. **Accept-All (Silver Lower Bound):** Ingests all 800 practice pool episodes without filtering.
3. **Kodiac (Cloud):** Full pipeline with SAM 3.1 segmentation and Muse Spark 1.3 adjudication (`stackcube.green`). Rejection rule: `CAN'T TELL` is treated as **rejected** (not admitted to training set).
4. **Kodiac (Open-Weights):** Pure local execution using open weights (SmolVLM / Qwen2.5-VL / SAM OSS).
5. **Kodiac + Prefix Salvage:** Truncates failed episodes at measured onset frame $t^*$, retaining valid pre-failure sub-trajectories ($\ge 1.0\text{ s}$) as partial demonstrations.
6. **Single-Pass Muse Spark 1.3 (2 fps):** Native VLM baseline prompted with task instructions and video sampled at 2 fps (binary PASS/FAIL).
7. **Robometer-4B:** Open-source robotic VLM judge (evaluation via standard prompt and reward threshold).
8. **TOPReward:** Trajectory-level policy reward model from LeRobot v0.6 (`lerobot.rewards`).
9. **Size-Matched Random Control:** Random subset of oracle-successful episodes sampled to match the exact episode count admitted by Kodiac ($|\mathcal{D}| = |\mathcal{D}_{\text{Kodiac}}|$).
10. **Oracle + $k\%$ Injected False-PASS:** Calibrated synthetic contamination doses with $k \in \{5\%, 10\%, 20\%, 30\%\}$ false-PASS labels injected into oracle data to quantify performance degradation curves.

## 5. Policy Retraining Protocol

* **Model Architecture:** State-based Diffusion Policy (1D temporal CNN with DDPM scheduler, 100 diffusion steps).
* **Training Data:** Clean expert demonstrations (20 episodes) $\cup$ Accepted practice pool episodes ($\mathcal{D}_{\text{accepted}}(J)$).
* **Optimization:** AdamW optimizer ($\text{lr} = 10^{-4}$), batch size 64, trained for exactly **20,000 gradient steps**.
* **Checkpoints:** Evaluated at 5,000, 10,000, 15,000, and 20,000 steps.
* **Seeds:** 3 fixed seeds (`42, 43, 44`) trained in parallel on Modal L4 GPUs.
* **Evaluation:** $\ge 200$ held-out environment seeds (`1001` through `1200`) per training seed.
* **Modal Compute Guards:** Max wall-clock time per training run $\le 20\text{ min}$; cumulative GPU budget $\le 12.0\text{ GPU-hours}$ on Modal L4.

## 6. Statistical Analysis & Exit Criteria

* **Binary Classification Metrics (on 800-episode Training Split):**
  * False-PASS rate = $\frac{\text{False Positives}}{\text{False Positives} + \text{True Negatives}}$
  * False-REJECT rate = $\frac{\text{False Negatives}}{\text{False Negatives} + \text{True Positives}}$
  * Coverage = $\frac{\text{Adjudicated Episodes (PASS or FAIL)}}{\text{Total Episodes}}$
  * 95% Confidence Intervals: Computed via Wilson score intervals for binomial proportions.
  * Balanced Accuracy and AUROC: Computed via 1,000-sample non-parametric episode bootstrap.
* **Downstream Comparison:**
  * Two-sided Welch's $t$-test across 3 seed means for $\Delta \text{Success} = \mu_{\text{Kodiac}} - \mu_{\text{Baseline}}$.
  * Holm-Bonferroni correction applied across the baseline comparisons.
* **Target Success Criteria:**
  * Engine Agreement on Sim: Balanced Accuracy $\ge 0.90$, Coverage $\ge 0.85$.
  * Downstream Advantage: $\Delta \text{Success}(\text{Kodiac} - \text{Spark}) \ge +5.0\%$ absolute gain ($p < 0.05$).

## 7. Stopping Rules & Pre-Registered Contingencies

1. **Weak Policy Quality Tripwire:** If the initial weak policy $\pi_0$ exhibits oracle success $< 20\%$ or $> 70\%$ on 100 validation rollouts, re-tune the number of demonstrations ($N \in [15, 35]$) or checkpoint steps before generating the pool.
2. **Missing Action Key Kill Criterion:** If any episode `.state.npz` lacks `actions` of shape `(T, 7)` in `pd_ee_delta_pos`, pool generation terminates immediately.
3. **Pre-Registration Tag Order Tripwire:** If `proof/pool.py` is invoked without git tag `prereg-v0` present on the repository commit, execution aborts with `RuntimeError`.
4. **Minimal Fallback Version:** If external baseline models (Robometer / TOPReward) encounter unresolvable dependency conflicts on Modal L4, evaluation falls back to: Oracle, Accept-All, Kodiac, Single-Pass Spark 1.3, and synthetic noise doses ($k \in \{5, 10, 20, 30\}$).
